ABOUT C.P. COMPANY

In 1971, Italian designer Massimo Osti founded Chester Perry, a brand best known for its screen-printed t-shirts. In 1978, it was renamed C.P. Company, marking the beginning of one of the most creative and influential eras in contemporary sportswear. For over 50 years, C.P. Company has pioneered a hybrid style that merged military, workwear, and sportswear influences, supported by ongoing research into cutting-edge fabrics and textile technologies. Among its most significant innovations is garment dyeing, first developed in the mid-'70s and still a defining hallmark of the brand's collections. This fusion of functional design and Italian textile innovation lies at the core of C.P. Company and continues to shape every garment that carries its name.
